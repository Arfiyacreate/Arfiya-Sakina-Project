DIGITAL FOOTPRINTS

Definition:

A digital footprints is a data that is left behind when users have been online



2 Types of Digital Footprints:

Active digital footprint:

An active digital footprint is where auser has deliberately shared information about himself either by using websites.

Passive Digital Footprint:

A passive digital footprint is made when information is collected from the user without the person knowing this is happening.
