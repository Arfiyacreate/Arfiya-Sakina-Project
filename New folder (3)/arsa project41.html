CYBERCRIME

Definition:

Cybercrime refers to any illegal activity that is carried out using computers mobile phones, or the internet

Some examples of cybercrimes:

Hacking:

Hacking is the unauthorized access to computer system or network to steal, change, or destroy information.



Phishing:

Phishing is a cybercrime in which fake emails or messages are sent to trick people into revealing personal information like passwords or bank details



Identity Theft

Identity Theft is the crime of stealing someone's personal information and using it to commit fraud or other crimes.
